BTD6 Monkey Team Generator

This is a tool to generate Monkey Teams for BTD6 (a.k.a. the events that sometimes appear on a map and challenge you to only use the three listed towers for extra Monkey Money).

I made this because when me and my boyfriend play co-op, sometimes we wanna do a Monkey Team challenge just for fun on a map that doesn't have the event on it.

Click the "Generate" button to generate a random team. You can toggle "Pick random path" if you want a random Tier 5 upgrade path generated for each monkey too.
